Orders

Review InstaPay, Card, and Cash on Delivery orders, and move each one along with Change Status: Confirmed, Shipped, Delivered, or one step back if something goes wrong. Confirm an InstaPay payment only after the money shows in your bank app. For a large order, agree the delivery fee with the customer on WhatsApp, then use Set Delivery Fee before you confirm it. Confirming takes the items out of stock; cancelling or going back to Waiting for Payment puts them back. Current stock comes from each product right now, not from the amounts stored when the order was placed.
