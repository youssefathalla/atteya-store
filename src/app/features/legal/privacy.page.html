Privacy Policy

Last updated: Oct 31, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Atteya Store (“we”, “us”) collects and uses personal information when you browse the Store, create an account, save favorites, or place an order.

Data we collect

  • Account data — such as name (when provided by your sign-in provider), email address, and authentication identifiers.
  • Order and delivery data — such as shipping name, address, phone number, order contents, and payment status (not full card numbers).
  • Favorites — product ids you save while signed in.
  • Marketing preference — whether you opted in to discount and similar emails, and when that choice was recorded.
  • Device and analytics data — such as browser type, approximate location used for currency display, pages viewed, and diagnostic logs needed to run and secure the Store.

Why we use your data

  • Create and secure your account, and keep you signed in on your devices.
  • Process orders, payments, shipping, cancellations, and customer support.
  • Save and show your favorites list.
  • Show prices in a suitable currency and keep the Store working reliably.
  • Send marketing emails only when you have opted in (see Marketing below).
  • Detect abuse, prevent fraud, and meet legal obligations.

Marketing

We send discount offers and similar emails only if you opt in (for example, by checking the box when you create an account). Marketing is never required to create an account or place an order.

You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in those emails, or by contacting us. Turning off marketing does not stop transactional messages about your orders or account.
