Payment

  • InstaPay (Egypt): you send the full total by InstaPay, send us the screenshot on WhatsApp, and we confirm your order once we see the payment.
  • Cash on Delivery (Egypt): you pay the delivery fee by InstaPay first and send us the screenshot. We then send the order, and you pay the rest in cash at your door. If delivery is free, there is nothing to send first.
  • Bank Transfer (outside Egypt): you send the total from your bank to our account and send us the receipt on WhatsApp. Please choose “I pay all fees” in your bank app so the full amount reaches us.

An order waits for payment until you pay or we cancel it. We don't cancel unpaid orders automatically.

Shipping

We pack your order in the smallest box that fits it, and the delivery fee depends on that box. You don't choose the box. The fee is shown at checkout before you place your order, and some orders get free delivery.

For very large or heavy orders in Egypt, we agree the delivery fee with you on WhatsApp after you place the order, and we confirm it once you agree. We can't deliver abroad orders that are heavier than our biggest box.

We'll update your order as it moves from Confirmed to Shipped to Delivered, and email you at each step. Delivery in Egypt usually takes 1 to 4 days after we ship your order. Delivery abroad takes longer and depends on the country. These are estimates, not promises.
