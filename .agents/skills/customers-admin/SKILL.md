## Customers

- **Customers data (<KEY>-276)**: `customers/{customerId}` holds one summary per email address (`customerId` = sha256 of the trimmed, lower-cased email, the `emailSignups` rule): everyone who placed an order (signed in or guest) or has an account; the admin Customers pages (<KEY>-277, <KEY>-278) read it and never add up orders in the browser.
- Fields: `email`, `name` / `phone` / `city` / `country` (newest order's shipping address, else the account's `users/{uid}`), `uid`, `hasAccount` (Auth user with a `users/{uid}` doc), `marketingOptIn`, `emailVerified`, `orderCount` (every order except Payment Failed), `openOrderCount` (Waiting for Payment), `cancelledOrderCount` (Cancelled + Payment Failed), `spentEgp` (`totalEgp` of Confirmed, Shipped and Delivered), `averageOrderEgp` (rounded), `firstOrderAt` / `lastOrderAt` (counted orders), `createdAt` (earliest order or account), `updatedAt`, `searchTokens` (`orderSearchTokens()` words for name, phone and email, plus up to 50 short order numbers) and `emailVariants` (other spellings orders saved the email with).
- Pure rules in `functions/src/customers/customer-summary.ts` (`buildCustomerSummary()`, `customerIdFromEmail()`; tests `customer-summary.test.ts`); triggers in `customer-summary.trigger.ts` (`europe-west3`, `retry: true`): `onOrderWrittenUpdateCustomer` (every order write; both emails when it changed), `onUserCreatedUpdateCustomer`, `onUserUpdatedUpdateCustomer` (only when `name`, `phone`, `address` or `marketingOptIn` changed) and `onUserDeletedUpdateCustomer` (finds the customer by `uid`, since the Auth user may be gone).
- Each trigger rebuilds the whole customer from the newest 500 orders with that email (`customerEmail in` the lower-cased email plus `emailVariants`, ordered by `createdAt`; index `orders` (`customerEmail`, `createdAt`)), never adding or subtracting, so retries are safe; the orders are read outside the transaction (no locks on orders), and a transaction on the customer doc writes only when that read is newer than its `builtFromReadTime`, so out-of-order events never leave older totals; order writes that change none of `CUSTOMER_ORDER_FIELDS` (status, total, shipping name / phone / city / country, email, `createdAt`) are skipped; a customer with no orders and no account left is deleted; `emailVerified` refreshes on the next order or account change.
- Rules: `customers` is read by `canManageStore()` and written only by Cloud Functions (left out of the catch-all); the list indexes for <KEY>-277 (`hasAccount`, `searchTokens` with `lastOrderAt` / `spentEgp` / `orderCount` / `createdAt` desc) are in `firestore.indexes.json`; the website schema is `CustomerSchema` + `customerConverter` (`@core/services/customers`).
- `functions/scripts/fill-customers.mjs` (dry run by default, `--apply` to write, prod also needs `--confirm-prod`; on dev the manager agent applies after a dry run, prod only on Youssef's OK) builds every customer from all orders and accounts with the same rules and prints how many it would create, change and remove (each write checks the doc's update time and counts a customer the triggers changed meanwhile instead of overwriting it); run it after `fb:rules`, `fb:indexes` and `fb:fn` deploys.
- **Admin Customers list (<KEY>-277)**: `/admin/customers` (sidebar Sales & Orders → **Customers**, staff and admins through `adminGuard`; `features/admin/pages/customers-manager/`) lists `customers` through `CustomersAdminService` (`services/customers-admin.service.ts`) with four summary boxes (Customers + new this month, Ordered more than once + share of customers who ordered, Average spent of customers with `spentEgp > 0`, With an account + guests), each a `getCountFromServer` / `getAggregateFromServer` read of its own (a failed one shows "—").
- The Customers list has **Search customers** (`orderSearchTerms()`; Firestore matches the longest word in `searchTokens`, the other words are checked against the saved `searchTokens` in the browser), **Sort by** (Last Order, Newest First (default) · Most Spent · Most Orders · Newest Customers), one filter chip at a time with counts (All · Ordered More Than Once · Accounts · Guests · Account, No Order Yet; a Material **Show** select below `@3xl`) and 50 customers at a time with **Show More Customers** (`startAfter` cursor); pure helpers in `customers-manager/utils/customers-list.utils.ts`, styles `.customer-*` in `admin-customers.css`.
- Customers list queries: Ordered More Than Once uses `orderCount >= 2` with the sort field (indexes `customers` (sort field desc, `orderCount` desc)); Account, No Order Yet is always newest customers first (every other sort is equal for them); with a search, Ordered More Than Once and the no-order part of Account, No Order Yet are checked in the browser and the line says "Showing 3 customers" with no total.
- Each Customers row links to `/admin/customers/:customerId`, the customer page (<KEY>-278).
- **Admin customer page (<KEY>-278)**: `/admin/customers/:customerId` (`customers-manager/customer-details/`) shows **All Customers**, a black header (first letter, name, Account / Guest and Returning (`orderCount >= 2`) pills, "email · phone · city", "Customer since … · Email confirmed · Gets offer emails" (the last two for accounts only), **Message on WhatsApp** (`whatsappChatUrl(shopperWhatsappNumber(phone, country))`, hidden when the number can't be converted) and **Copy Phone**), 4 boxes (Orders, Spent, Average order, Last order), the Orders card, Delivery Addresses and Staff Note; an unknown id shows `<app-error-page>` Page Not Found, a failed read Try Again.
- The customer page's "3 completed · 1 open · 1 cancelled" line has no stored completed count: completed = `round(spentEgp / averageOrderEgp)` (capped at `orderCount - openOrderCount`), cancelled = the rest of `orderCount` (Payment Failed is not in `orderCount`) (`orderMixLine()` in `customer-details/utils/customer-details.utils.ts`).
- The customer page's Orders card reads `orders` where `customerEmail in` the lower-cased email plus `emailVariants` (`orderEmailValues()`), `createdAt` desc, 20 at a time with **Show More Orders** (index `orders` (`customerEmail`, `createdAt` desc)), parsing each order on its own (one that doesn't parse is left out) and leaving out Payment Failed, like `orderCount`; each row opens `/admin/orders/:orderId`; status pills are black for Confirmed, Shipped and Delivered, outlined for Waiting for Payment and light grey for Cancelled and Payment Failed (`.customer-status`).
- Delivery Addresses groups the shipping addresses of the orders loaded so far (Payment Failed included; more appear after Show More Orders) (same street, city, region, postal code and country, case and spaces ignored; newest use first, at most 5, `customerAddresses()`), with the account's `users/{uid}.address` first even when no order used it ("Saved on the account · used 4 times", "Used once · order #A1B2C3").
- **Staff note (<KEY>-278)**: `customerNotes/{customerId}` (`note`, `updatedAt`, `updatedBy`, `updatedByEmail`; `CustomerNoteSchema` in `@core/services/customers`) is read and written from the customer page by `canManageStore()`; the rules allow only those four fields, `note` a string of at most 1,000 characters, `updatedAt == request.time`, `updatedBy == request.auth.uid` and `updatedByEmail` equal to the token's email (`''` without one), never delete, and `customerNotes` is left out of the catch-all.
- The Customer box on `/admin/orders/:orderId` and in the open order of the Orders list links the name to that customer's page (`customerIdForEmail()` in `@core/services/customers`: SHA-256 of the trimmed, lower-cased email with Web Crypto, the same id as `customerIdFromEmail()` in functions); an order with no email shows the name without a link.
