## Order emails

- **Order emails (Resend)**: `sendOrderEmails` (`functions/src/email/send-order-emails.trigger.ts`, `onDocumentWritten orders/{orderId}`, `europe-west3`, no retry) sends English-only emails through the Resend REST API (`resend-client.ts`, no SDK). Which email: `orderEmailsForWrite()` in `order-email-events.ts` — cash on delivery placed → `cod-placed` + `store-new-order`; cash on delivery pending → confirmed → `cod-confirmed`; InstaPay placed → `instapay-placed` + `store-new-order`; InstaPay pending → confirmed → `instapay-confirmed`; cancelling sends nothing; reaching Shipped the first time → `order-shipped`, Delivered the first time → `order-delivered` (any payment method; a step back sends nothing and never a second confirmation); Paymob → confirmed → `paid-confirmed` + `store-new-order`; Paymob → failed → `payment-failed`; a large order's delivery fee saved for the first time → `delivery-fee-set`, changed while still open → `delivery-fee-changed` (placed emails and the store alert say the fee is to be agreed instead of showing it). A pending Paymob order sends nothing. `instapay-placed` and `cod-placed` (fee above 0) carry an InstaPay box (amount, address/number, **Send Screenshot on WhatsApp** button with the shopper's Arabic text) built only from `paymentDue()`; `OrderEmailContext.instapay` comes from `STORE_INFO.payments.instapay`. Email headings, labels and buttons use the same title case as website buttons ("New Order", "Delivery Address", "View Your Order", "Open This Order"); sentences stay sentence case. Wording is in `order-email-templates.ts` (pure, unit tested in `order-emails.spec.ts`) and on the "Order emails with Resend" board task; amounts are whole EGP from the saved order only (no converted currencies), shipping 0 shows "Free", shopper emails never repeat the phone number, and abroad paid emails say the charge was in EGP. Each (order, email) is claimed in `orderEmails/{orderId}_{kind}` (`create()`, then `sent` or `failed` with the error) so a repeated trigger never sends twice; a failure is not retried automatically. `orderEmails` is admin-read, Admin-SDK-write only. Secret `RESEND_API_KEY` (Secret Manager); params `RESEND_FROM` (default Resend test sender, which only delivers to the Resend account owner), `RESEND_REPLY_TO` (optional; enables "just reply" copy), `STORE_ALERT_EMAIL` (empty = no alert), plus `STOREFRONT_URL` for links. Photos use ImageKit (`/dev` folder on `atteya-store-dev`). The header name and the business WhatsApp come from `STORE_INFO`; when `contact.whatsapp` is set, shopper emails end with a black footer ("Questions? We answer on WhatsApp." and a **Message Us on WhatsApp** button) linking to `wa.me/<digits>` instead of the reply line. The sender is `support@atteyastore.com` (domain `atteyastore.com` in Resend, region `eu-west-1`; its DNS is at Dynadot and the inbox is Zoho, so the Resend records live on the `send` and `resend._domainkey` names and never touch the root MX), with `RESEND_REPLY_TO` the same address.
- **Shopper email design ("Bold Black", <KEY>-221)**: every shopper order email and Confirm Your Email is built from `functions/src/email/email-blocks.ts`: a black header (`ATTEYA STORE` letter-spaced, the order ref on the right, a small grey kicker such as `CASH ON DELIVERY`, a huge two-line uppercase status such as `ORDER PLACED`, one sentence, and on confirmed / shipped / delivered a Placed → Confirmed → Shipped → Delivered bar), a big amount block, numbered steps, one full-width black button, "Your Items", totals under a 3px black rule, Delivery To / Payment grey boxes, "View Your Order →" and a black footer.
- Email HTML is email-safe: tables and inline styles only (no flex, grid or `position`), 600px wide, system font stack, buttons as padded `<a>` links, square corners except the round step circles, monochrome only (`#111111`, white, greys `#f4f4f4` / `#ededed` / `#e5e5e5`, text `#555555` / `#6b6b6b`); one `<style>` media query only tightens padding on phones.
- The shopper emails' steps and InstaPay / bank boxes come only from `paymentDue()` (`dueFor()` in `order-email-templates.ts`); without InstaPay or bank details the step says we'll send them and no screenshot step shows; without `contact.whatsapp` every WhatsApp button is left out and the footer says "Questions? Just reply to this email." when `RESEND_REPLY_TO` is set.
- Items: up to 4 lines as 2 photos per row (a free gift is a slim strip with a black `FREE GIFT` tag under the item it comes with, never a card of its own), 5 or more items as a compact list (64px photo, price on the right); never more than 2 photos per row in any email (`itemGrid()` and `photoStrip()` in `email-blocks.ts`); Shipped shows 2 per row without prices, Delivered a row of up to 2 photos edge to edge with the names under it; Payment failed lists only bought items under "Still Want These?".
- Every email photo (Your Items, Pack List, Shipped, Delivered row) is fitted inside a fixed-height grey table cell (`photo()` in `email-blocks.ts`, `max-width` / `max-height`, never cropped), so names and prices line up whatever the photo's shape; the Delivered photo row uses percent widths so it never makes the page wider than a phone, and below 480px the staff amounts strip stacks one amount per line; the bank box breaks the IBAN only between groups of 4.
- On phones (below 480px) the two-amount block (Pay Now / At Your Door) stacks one amount above the other (`.half` in `emailDocument()`), so a 6-digit cash amount never makes the email wider than 375px (<KEY>-224).
- InstaPay, Card and Bank Transfer confirmations share one email (`paidConfirmed()`, `PAID BY …` with a ✓ circle); Shipped and Delivered kickers carry the date from the order's `shippedAt` / `deliveredAt` (`shippedAtMs` / `deliveredAtMs` in `order-email-model.ts`, Cairo time, e.g. `SHIPPED 14 NOV`).
- Every shopper email keeps a plain-text version with the same facts in the same order (header, amount, numbered steps with the box lines, button, items, totals, boxes, link, help line).
- **Staff new-order alert ("Bold Black", <KEY>-222)**: `store-new-order` (`storeNewOrder()` in `order-email-templates.ts`, staff blocks `staffHeader`, `statStrip`, `nextStepBox`, `outlinedButton` in `email-blocks.ts`) has a black header (`ATTEYA STORE` with an outlined `STAFF` tag, kicker `NEW ORDER · 12 NOV · 14:32` in Cairo time from `createdAt`, the total very large (Bank Transfer: the `payIn` amount with the EGP total under it), `#A1B2C3 · <method>`), a strip of ITEMS (pieces) plus the amounts from `storePayment()`, a grey next-step box (action in bold, then `storePayment().note`; none when there is no note), the customer (name, `tel:` phone · email, address · Guest checkout or Account · box, outlined **Message Customer on WhatsApp** from `staffWhatsappText()` / `shopperWhatsappNumber()`), a Pack List (2 photos per row with a black `×2` tag, a free gift as a strip under its item, 5 or more items as a compact list), totals, a full-width **Open This Order** to `/admin/orders/<orderId>` and a black footer "Sent to the store team for every new order."; the subject lines never change.
- In the staff alert, `needsReview` turns the header kicker into `CHECK THIS ORDER` with "One item sold out before this payment came in. Please check this order." under the ref, and a large order (`feeToBeAgreed`) into `AGREE THE DELIVERY FEE` with the heavier-than-the-biggest-box note (said once, so its next-step box shows only the action).
- Every order email's "View Your Order" link is `orderConfirmationUrl()` (`functions/src/email/order-email-model.ts`): a guest order's link carries `?key={viewKey}`, a signed-in order's link has no key.
- Return and exchange emails (`sendReturnEmails`, `returnEmails/{requestId}_{kind}` claims, `store-return-request` and `return-requested`) are described in the `returns-and-exchanges` skill; they share `emailContext()` (`functions/src/email/email-context.ts`) with the order emails, and `statStrip()` cells may carry a small note under the amount.
